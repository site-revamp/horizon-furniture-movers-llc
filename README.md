Horizon Furniture Movers LLC
1. Website Overview
Horizon Furniture Movers LLC is a Dubai-based moving and relocation company providing residential moving, commercial relocation, professional packing, and storage solutions.

The redesigned homepage targets homeowners, tenants, families, and businesses looking for organised relocation services in Dubai. Its primary purpose is to communicate the company's services more clearly, explain the moving process, build customer confidence, and encourage quotation requests.

The new design focuses on clearer service positioning, improved content structure, and accessible enquiry options across desktop and mobile devices.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Business Positioning and Differentiation
Previous issue: The original homepage relied on generic positioning and did not communicate the company's services and value clearly enough.

Implemented solution:
- Introduced a customer-focused hero section.
- Improved the primary messaging to communicate furniture moving and relocation services.
- Created clearer, more coordinated service messaging.
Result: Visitors can understand the company's core offerings more quickly.
2. Improved Service Discovery and Customer Guidance
Previous issue: Service information and customer decision support were insufficiently structured.

Implemented solution:
- Improved residential and commercial service discovery.
- Introduced six custom service icons to make service categories easier to recognise.
- Added a four-stage moving journey to explain the relocation process.
Result: Customers can identify relevant services and better understand how the moving process works.
3. Stronger Enquiry and Conversion Pathways
Previous issue: The homepage lacked sufficiently prominent quotation pathways and clear guidance for customers wanting to make an enquiry.

Implemented solution:
- Added prominent quotation enquiry pathways.
- Improved moving enquiry guidance.
